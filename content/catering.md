## Catering Packages & Occasions

### 1. Boardroom & Corporate Lunches
* **Ideal for**: Executive meetings, office team lunches, quarterly reviews & client appreciation.
* **Service**: Hot saffron basmati trays, twin charbroiled Koobideh skewers, Sabzi Khordan, and fresh pita delivered boardroom-ready with chafing racks.

![Corporate Team Feast](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1556761175-b413da4baf72?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=85)

### 2. Family Dinners & Home Celebrations
* **Ideal for**: Nowruz, birthdays, anniversaries & family gatherings.
* **Service**: Full Persian family-style table setups — slow-braised Ghormeh Sabzi, Fesenjan, Kashk-e Bademjan starters, and golden tahdig.

![Family Feast Table](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1529543544282-ea669407fca3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=85)

### 3. Backyard Parties & Friends Get-Togethers
* **Ideal for**: Summer backyard parties, friend reunions & outdoor celebrations.
* **Service**: On-site live mesquite charcoal grill masters bringing sizzling Joojeh skewers, grilled tomatoes, and ice-cold mint Doogh right to your backyard.

![Backyard Party Feast](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1528605248644-14dd04022da1?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=85)

### 4. Weddings & Royal Galas
* **Ideal for**: Weddings, gala receptions & grand banquets.
* **Service**: Royal Soltani platters, Zereshk Polo, Bastani saffron ice cream stations, and elaborate Persian dessert towers.
